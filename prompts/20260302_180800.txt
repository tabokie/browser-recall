Fundamentally, deletion affects user in two ways: viewing checkpoints of page and lists, viewing search results. History is immutable by design, therefore we don't support deletion that may affect first-order search results. That means we cannot delete information inlined in history logs, including page visits and inlined notes/highlights. What we can delete, is second-order entities and relations. That includes lists, page relations, captures. These can be applied once and for all without later filtering.

One minor issue is, we want user to have a consistent mental model around note deletion. Therefore maybe it is better to always store note/highlights seperately, like captures?
