Keep tests organized around two test types:

- E2E tests are the primary coverage style. They exercise product workflows through the real filesystem and real Git wherever the behavior crosses storage, persistence, or versioning boundaries. Put them under `Tests/JournalE2ETests/`.

Prefer adding new workflow coverage as E2E first. Add property/state tests when the behavior has many edge cases, benefits from deterministic fuzzing, or would be too slow or brittle with real external boundaries.
