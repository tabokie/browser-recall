# browser-recall — Chrome Web Store Listing

## Short description (132 chars max)

Save your browsing history, notes, and highlights to a local folder you control. Your data stays on your device.

## Detailed description

browser-recall saves your browsing history, notes, and highlights to a local folder you choose — not to the cloud, not to a database, just plain files on your computer.

**Local-first storage**
Pick any folder on your device. All data is stored as readable JSON and JSONL files. Your data folder is your backup — copy it, version it, or read it with any text editor.

**Automatic history tracking**
Every page you visit is recorded with its URL, title, and visit time. Revisits are detected automatically, so you can see how many times you've been to a page and when.

**Full-text search**
Search across all your recorded pages by title, URL, or content. Results appear instantly thanks to a local WASM-powered search index.

**Notes and highlights**
Attach notes to any page. Highlight text on any page and it's saved for later. Your annotations travel with you — visible whenever you revisit the page.

**Page snapshots**
Capture a full offline copy of any page with one click or a keyboard shortcut (Alt+S). View saved snapshots anytime, even without an internet connection.

**Lists and organization**
Create custom lists to organize pages. Pin important pages. Like or dislike pages to surface what matters.

**Smart rules**
Set up automatic rules to categorize pages based on URL patterns or keywords. Pages matching your rules are tagged and organized without manual effort.

**Multi-device sync**
Optionally sync your data across devices using GitHub, a shared filesystem, or WebDAV. You control where your data goes — browser-recall never sends it anywhere on its own.

**Keyboard shortcuts**
- Alt+S — Capture snapshot
- Alt+H — Highlight selected text
- Alt+L — Like current page
- Alt+D — Dislike current page

**Privacy by design**
No analytics. No tracking. No accounts. No data collection. Everything stays on your device unless you explicitly set up sync to a service you control.
